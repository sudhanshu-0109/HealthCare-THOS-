## 2. Detailed Exercise Breakdown & Form Specifications

### 1. Hip Thrust (Featured Pinterest Demonstration)
- **Primary Muscle Group:** Gluteus Maximus
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Core, Quadriceps
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight / Bench / Resistance Band / Dumbbell
- **Direct GIF Link:** `https://i.pinimg.com/originals/12/31/e2/1231e24fc3d7944e38b180c51067689d.gif`
- **Local Fallback Asset:** `/exercises/hip-thrust.gif`
- **Form & Execution:**
  1. Position your upper back against a sturdy bench or padded elevation, directly below the shoulder blades.
  2. Plant feet flat on the ground, shoulder-width apart, with shins vertical at full hip extension.
  3. Drive through your heels, engaging glutes to drive hips upward until your torso and thighs form a straight line parallel to the ground.
  4. Squeeze glutes forcefully at the apex for 1–2 seconds. Avoid hyperextending the lower back.
  5. Lower hips under control without resting completely on the floor, and repeat.
- **Pinterest Search Terms:** `hip thrust exercise gif animation`, `glute bridge workout gif illustration`

### 2. Jumping Jacks
- **Primary Muscle Group:** Cardiovascular, Full Body
- **Secondary Muscles:** Calves, Glutes, Deltoids
- **Direct GIF Link:** `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/omercotkd/exercises-gifs/main/assets/3224.gif`
- **Form & Execution:**
  1. Start in an upright standing position with feet together and arms at your sides.
  2. Jump outward, landing softly on the balls of your feet with legs wider than shoulder-width, while sweeping arms in an arc overhead.
  3. Immediately spring back to starting position with feet together and arms lowered.
- **Pinterest Search Terms:** `jumping jacks workout gif`, `cardio warm up animation gif`

### 3. Hip Circles
- **Primary Muscle Group:** Hip Joint Mobility, Hip Flexors
- **Secondary Muscles:** Lower Back, Abdominals
- **Direct GIF Link:** `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/omercotkd/exercises-gifs/main/assets/1368.gif`
- **Form & Execution:**
  1. Stand upright with hands on hips and feet shoulder-width apart.
  2. Rotate your pelvis in wide, controlled circles in a clockwise direction for 30 seconds.
  3. Reverse direction to counterclockwise for the remaining 30 seconds to mobilize both hips evenly.
- **Pinterest Search Terms:** `hip circles mobility warm up gif`, `pelvic rotation exercise animation`

### 4. Plank Hold
- **Primary Muscle Group:** Rectus Abdominis, Transverse Abdominis
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Glutes, Latissimus Dorsi
- **Direct GIF Link:** `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/omercotkd/exercises-gifs/main/assets/0464.gif`
- **Form & Execution:**
  1. Rest on forearms with elbows aligned directly under shoulders.
  2. Extend legs behind with toes tucked. Ensure the body forms a rigid straight line from crown to heels.
  3. Tuck tailbone slightly, brace core as if anticipating a punch, and breathe steadily.
- **Pinterest Search Terms:** `forearm plank exercise gif`, `core stability animation gif`

### 5. Mountain Climbers
- **Primary Muscle Group:** Core & Hip Flexors
- **Secondary Muscles:** Anterior Shoulders, Quadriceps, Calves
- **Direct GIF Link:** `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/omercotkd/exercises-gifs/main/assets/0630.gif`
- **Form & Execution:**
  1. Start in a high plank position with hands under shoulders and arms straight.
  2. Drive one knee toward your chest without touching the floor with your toes.
  3. Return leg back to plank while driving the opposite knee forward in a rhythmic running cadence.
- **Pinterest Search Terms:** `mountain climbers exercise gif`, `high plank cardio animation`

### 6. Russian Twists
- **Primary Muscle Group:** Obliques (Internal & External)
- **Secondary Muscles:** Rectus Abdominis, Hip Flexors
- **Direct GIF Link:** `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/omercotkd/exercises-gifs/main/assets/0687.gif`
- **Form & Execution:**
  1. Sit on a mat with knees bent at 90 degrees. Lean back slightly to approximately 45 degrees, creating a V-shape with thighs.
  2. Clasp hands together in front of chest.
  3. Rotate torso slowly from side to side, touching the floor adjacent to your hip with control.
- **Pinterest Search Terms:** `russian twists core workout gif`, `oblique twist exercise illustration`

### 7. High Knees
- **Primary Muscle Group:** Hip Flexors & Cardiovascular Endurance

### 8. Dead Bug
- **Primary Muscle Group:** Deep Transverse Abdominis (Anti-Extension)
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hip Flexors, Shoulders
- **Direct GIF Link:** `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/omercotkd/exercises-gifs/main/assets/0276.gif`
- **Form & Execution:**
  1. Lie flat on your back, raising arms straight toward ceiling and knees bent 90 degrees over hips.
  2. Press your lumbar spine firmly into the floor (no gap under lower back).
  3. Slowly lower your right arm overhead and left leg toward floor simultaneously.
  4. Return to center and alternate with left arm and right leg.
- **Pinterest Search Terms:** `dead bug exercise core gif`, `anti extension abdominal animation`

### 9. Burpees (Modified)
- **Primary Muscle Group:** Full Body / Metabolic Conditioning
- **Secondary Muscles:** Pectorals, Core, Quadriceps, Deltoids
- **Direct GIF Link:** `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/omercotkd/exercises-gifs/main/assets/1160.gif`
- **Form & Execution:**
  1. Begin standing. Drop into a squat and place hands flat on the ground.
  2. Step or jump feet back into a high plank.
  3. Step or jump feet back to hands, rise to standing, and perform a small jump with arms reaching overhead.
- **Pinterest Search Terms:** `burpees fitness workout gif`, `hiit exercise animation illustration`

### 11. Bodyweight Squats
- **Primary Muscle Group:** Quadriceps, Gluteus Maximus
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Adductors, Core
- **Direct GIF Link:** `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/omercotkd/exercises-gifs/main/assets/3222.gif`
- **Form & Execution:**
  1. Stand with feet slightly wider than shoulder-width, toes turned 15 degrees out.
  2. Initiate movement by hinging hips back and bending knees as if sitting into a chair.
  3. Descend until thighs are at least parallel to floor, keeping chest upright.
  4. Drive through heels to return to standing position.
- **Pinterest Search Terms:** `bodyweight squat workout gif`, `air squat animation guide`

## 3. UI Display & Orientation Rules in Frontend Code

To ensure the GIF animations render in **perfect orientation** without distortion or cropping on both desktop and mobile screens, the following CSS rules are implemented:

```jsx
// Dedicated Exercise Showcase Viewport (WorkoutSessionPage.jsx)
<div className="relative w-full h-56 sm:h-64 rounded-3xl bg-white border border-[var(--border)] overflow-hidden flex items-center justify-center shadow-sm">
  <img
    src={ex.gifUrl}
    alt={ex.name}
    className="w-full h-full object-contain p-2 mix-blend-multiply select-none pointer-events-none"
    loading="eager"
  />
</div>
```

### Key Properties:
1. **`object-contain`**: Preserves original aspect ratio without cropping, stretching, or squishing any axis.
2. **`mix-blend-multiply`**: Smoothly integrates light-background GIFs into the white showcase container.
3. **`p-2`**: Prevents the character or equipment from touching container edges.
4. **`h-56 sm:h-64`**: Fixed height ratio with fluid width ensures consistent viewport sizing across devices while the countdown timer runs directly below.

To expand the library with new exercises:

1. **Pinterest Method:**
   - Search formula: `[Exercise Name] exercise gif animation illustration`
   - Example: `Bulgarian split squat exercise gif animation`
   - Open image in new tab to obtain the direct `https://i.pinimg.com/originals/...` URL.

   - The backup host contains 1,324 indexed bodyweight and gym exercise animations at:
     `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/omercotkd/exercises-gifs/main/assets/[ID].gif`
   - All assets support cross-origin resource sharing (`Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`).
3. **Adding to App:**
   - Open `frontend/src/data/physicalWellnessMockData.js`.
   - Add `{ name, description, sets, reps, duration, rest, instructions, gifUrl }` into `mockTodayWorkout`.
